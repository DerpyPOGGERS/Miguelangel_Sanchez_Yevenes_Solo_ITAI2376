# Smart HVAC Support Knowledge Base

## Not cooling
Ask if the thermostat is set to COOL and at least 3 degrees below room temperature. Ask the customer to check the air filter and the breaker. Do not tell the customer to open electrical panels or handle refrigerant. Recommend a technician if the system still does not cool.

## Water leak or dripping
A small drip can come from a clogged drain line, dirty filter, frozen coil, or damaged drain pan. The customer can safely turn the system off and place a towel or pan under the drip. Recommend service if dripping continues, there is ceiling damage, or water is near electrical equipment.

## No heat
Ask if the thermostat is set to HEAT and if the filter is clean. If the customer smells gas, tell them to leave the area, avoid switches or flames, and call the gas company or emergency services. Recommend a technician for furnace issues.

## Strange noise
Rattling, grinding, buzzing, or loud vibration can mean loose panels, blower motor problems, contactor issues, or debris. Ask when the noise happens and whether it is indoor or outdoor. Recommend a technician if the noise is loud or repeated.

## Seasonal maintenance
Seasonal checks help catch dirty coils, clogged drains, weak capacitors, low airflow, and safety issues before peak summer or winter. Ask the customer for preferred days and time windows.

## Filters
Ask for the filter size before bringing filters. Common sizes include 20x20x1, 20x25x1, and 25x25x1. A dirty filter can reduce airflow and cause cooling or heating problems.

## Appointment scheduling policy
Offer morning window 8 AM to 12 PM or afternoon window 12 PM to 4 PM. For urgent issues, recommend the earliest available service window and flag for a technician.
